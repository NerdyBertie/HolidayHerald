# Holiday Herald

## v1.5.0

### New
- Moonkin Festival (November 12) now has a micro-holiday card: how to earn all five Moonkin Hatchlings, with coordinates, plus two secrets.
- Wanderer's Festival (early December) now has a micro-holiday card: ceremony times, the achievement, the turtle pet, and two secrets.
- New Year's Fireworks (December 31) now has a micro-holiday card: show times, ways to make the fireworks pop, party favors, and two secrets.

### Improvements
- Pilgrim's Bounty: Now We're Cookin' and Pilgrim's Progress now count the right version for your faction.
- Pilgrim's Bounty tip: leveling Classic Cooking from 1 to 300, the Spice Bread recipe, where to find Wild Turkeys, and every feast table location.
- Two new Pilgrim's Bounty secrets.
- The Great Gnomeregan Run's title now fades from gnome pink to tinker teal, like the other holidays.

## v1.4.0

### New
- Feast of Winter Veil is now a full card: the Merrymaker meta, 31 toys, 10 pets, a mount, holiday transmog and hats, a shopping list for Greatfather Winter's treats, the hunter reindeer, and seven secrets.

### Improvements
- The popup now shows the version number next to its title.
- Hallow's End: all nine Horseman's Ghoulish pieces and the whole Patched Harvest Golem set are now tracked individually, plus Kickin' With the Wick and The Lick King.
- Hallow's End tip: Wickerman buffs, the Shade of the Horseman event, and better hard mode details.
- Two new Hallow's End secrets, and more detail for the Coin of Many Faces and witch hat secrets.
- Day of the Dead: the Bouquet of Orange Marigolds and Whimsical Skull Mask are now tracked, the tip lists each race's home graveyard with coordinates, and there's a new Demon Hunter secret.

## v1.3.0

### New
- Achievement toasts: a tea-sipping herald pops up with a little cheer whenever you earn any achievement. Turn it off under Celebrations in the settings.
- Trading Post reminder under Weekly Events, with your Trader's Tender. Hover for where to find it, or click to open the Traveler's Log.

### Improvements
- Toasts now wait their turn, so several achievements at once don't overlap.
- Trading Post hover lists all four locations, including Silvermoon and Dornogal.
- Tooltips with map coordinates now mention TomTom's /way command.

## v1.2.0

### New
- Darkmoon Faire is now a full card: 31 achievements, 4 mounts, 19 toys, 22 pets, and 14 transmog pieces, each checked against your collection.
- Mounts now get their own count line on holiday cards.
- Three new Darkmoon Faire secrets.

### Improvements
- Darkmoon Faire tip: the WHEE! XP and reputation buff, the Darkmoon Top Hat, the Dance Dance Darkmoon toy, and the Blight Boar concert.
- With three or more holiday cards, the popup switches to two side-by-side columns, so it doesn't get so tall.
- The popup can't be dragged off screen anymore, and there's a new "Reset" button for its position in the settings (or type /herald resetpos).

### Fixes
- The Addon Workshop list in Options now always shows, no matter which NerdyBertie addon's settings you opened first.
- Card borders no longer disappear on some sides at certain UI scales.

## v1.1.1

### Improvements
- The "Hover any line for details" hint now sits at the top of the popup, next to the Holidays heading.

## v1.1.0

### New
- New holidays: Day of the Dead, Pilgrim's Bounty, WoW's Anniversary, and The Great Gnomeregan Run.
- Every other holiday now has a themed card, with full details coming before each one arrives.
- ItemWatch support: Ctrl-click a shopping line to add the whole list to ItemWatch, on top of any goals you already have.
- Shopping lists only show what your professions need, like the Darkmoon Faire profession quests.
- Micro-holidays can have their own cards.
- The NerdyBertie page in Options > AddOns is now NerdyBertie's Addon Workshop, with a mascot and a list of my other addons (the ones you have installed are marked).

### Improvements
- Tidier cards: sections for Still to get, How to, and Secrets, with finished categories folded into one "Done" line.
- New color palette, with gradient and striped titles and separate border colors.
- Click any achievement line to open it, or shift-click to link it in chat.
- Proper square icons on every holiday card.
- Pirates' Day: The Captain's Booty, Ol' Eary's Insult Book, new transmog, and a new secret.

### Fixes
- Secret teasers now start with a capital letter.
- Shift-clicking a shopping line no longer starts over from the first item after the popup refreshes.
- Micro-holidays no longer show the "(short!)" tag.

## v1.0.1

### Improvements
- Timewalking events now show which expansion they're from.
- Hover any weekly event to see its calendar description.
- Presets are now a dropdown that shows which one is active (or "Custom").
- Presets no longer hide the minimap button.
- Hallow's End: the witch hat secret now tells you where to find the quest.

## v1.0.0

- First release! Themed holiday cards, collection tracking, holiday boss info, meta achievement links, tips, secrets, class lines, short-holiday alerts, celebration toasts, weekly events, and a settings panel.
