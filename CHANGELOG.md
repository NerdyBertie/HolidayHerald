# Holiday Herald

## v1.1.1

### Improvements
- The "Hover any line for details" hint now sits at the top of the popup, next to the Holidays heading.

## v1.1.0

### New
- New holidays: Day of the Dead, Pilgrim's Bounty, WoW's Anniversary, and The Great Gnomeregan Run.
- Every other holiday now has a themed card, with full details coming before each one arrives.
- ItemWatch support: Ctrl-click a shopping line to add the whole list to ItemWatch, on top of any goals you already have.
- Shopping lists only show what your professions need, like the Darkmoon Faire profession quests.
- Micro-holidays can have their own cards.
- The NerdyBertie page in Options > AddOns is now NerdyBertie's Addon Workshop, with a mascot and a list of my other addons (the ones you have installed are marked).

### Improvements
- Tidier cards: sections for Still to get, How to, and Secrets, with finished categories folded into one "Done" line.
- New color palette, with gradient and striped titles and separate border colors.
- Click any achievement line to open it, or shift-click to link it in chat.
- Proper square icons on every holiday card.
- Pirates' Day: The Captain's Booty, Ol' Eary's Insult Book, new transmog, and a new secret.

### Fixes
- Secret teasers now start with a capital letter.
- Shift-clicking a shopping line no longer starts over from the first item after the popup refreshes.
- Micro-holidays no longer show the "(short!)" tag.

## v1.0.1

### Improvements
- Timewalking events now show which expansion they're from.
- Hover any weekly event to see its calendar description.
- Presets are now a dropdown that shows which one is active (or "Custom").
- Presets no longer hide the minimap button.
- Hallow's End: the witch hat secret now tells you where to find the quest.

## v1.0.0

- First release! Themed holiday cards, collection tracking, holiday boss info, meta achievement links, tips, secrets, class lines, short-holiday alerts, celebration toasts, weekly events, and a settings panel.
